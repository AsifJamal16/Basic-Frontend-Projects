Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India • People's President • Bharat Ratna

Biography

Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played a major role in India’s civilian space program and military missile development. He is also known as the Missile Man of India due to his contribution to developing ballistic missiles and launch vehicle technology.

Timeline of Life

  • 1931: Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu
  • 1969: Joined ISRO
  • 1998: Played major role in Pokhran-II Nuclear Tests
  • 2002: Became the 11th President of India
  • 2015: Passed away during a lecture at IIM Shillong

“Dream is not that which you see while sleeping, it is something that does not let you sleep.”

Dr. Kalam inspired millions with his books, lectures, and vision for India’s future. He believed education, science, and youth are the pillars of a strong nation.
